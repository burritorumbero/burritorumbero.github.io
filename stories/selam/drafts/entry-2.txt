I am taller than average, which means that seeing the top of people's heads is part of everyday life.

And yet, when I look through a plane's window as it starts to take off, and I spot the top of a stranger's head, it kind of feels odd.

Then queue in the top of cars, the top of buildings and lastly the top of clouds, we all get taller, collectively speaking. 

But it's only the first part that feels strange, I don't know why. 

Maybe I am just worried of missing this place, even if for a brief moment. 

It's my first time doing a solo one-way sayonara unlikely-return type of trip, so it's not as if I could just turn to Mom or Juls and show them a funny video, you know?

Sis will be waiting on Posbon with a car to take me to a brand new apartment. Or that's what the insiders aka Vicky Code L have let go after some tedious field work I conducted over Rype calls. 

Apartment aside, I really wanna see campus, with the computer lab, the mechanic warehouse, the anime club, the MCA chapter. Heck! No need to pay me, even the soccer field seems interesting. 

If I get up early enough, I may end up surprising Ken on his debut match. 

NUR, here I come!
