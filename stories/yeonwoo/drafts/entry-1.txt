Today, I was again a bit sad that Yuto is no longer on school. That is why I brought a new book with me
this morning. Although I get lost on some chapters, the book is still entertaining. Yuto's sister told me
about it, and I am glad I listened. Reading with the breeze from the rooftop is very relaxing. 

However, because the summer is approaching, I have had to move around the rooftop to make sure I
grab a space with some shadow. There is days when the covered spaces are limited, so when the bell
rings, I have to leave the classroom running. Today I grabbed the last spot. 

A new girl on my class did not have much luck, and that made me feel bad, so I pretended I received
a message on my phone and stood up.

Maybe I stood up too abruptly, because when we looked at each other, she looked scared. I didn't say "Hi"
or anything. I think I am shy. So I just walked towards the door to let her know she could take my place.

I hope the new girl can make friends in the class. Being new anywhere is a scary thing.
