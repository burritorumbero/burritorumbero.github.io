New week, new cloudy skies, new cars on the parking lot. When I walked away from my SUV, however, I quickly realized that, next to a new vehicle, there was the extremely recurrent Evangeline B. Almost crouching to face one the windows, her voice didn't correspond to either a librarian or an up-and-coming tennis player. Profiting off my rights as a public space user, I payed closer attention while slowing my pace. "[...] and you said you'd try to have them relocate you. What did you do all this time over there then?" My boss seemed to be talking to a car window that's rapidly rolling up. "Can't you be patient for once? I said we'll discuss it tonight" the car replied to Evangeline.

"But you bring it up on our way here? Seriously, honey? You ar---" my boss stops herself by pulling her fingers from the passenger window. "What's wrong with you today." Her voice was that of an angry tall, very tall, person. I tried to focus on the driver, but it was too sunny and the windows were tinted. All I wanted was to not shift my gaze towards Evangeline and create ideas on my head regarding her persona. Something about being too much for certain activities, and all other sort of complaints she had made in the tennis court against society and its prejudices, refrained me from pointing my eyes straight at her.

The antagonizing car left the parking lot alongside me. Even if I elaborated the most compelling story behind my snoopy behavior, and even if I was certain of dissipating all skepticism with such a story, the necessity of turning around, looking up at my boss' face, and attaching an expression to her angered sounds, remained a stone too large to budge. I beat you to the office today, I would start when she comes into the library. Yeah, I just met the other library assistant, I would advance the one-way conversation. I am going to the David Scott concert, I would transition and remove my and other people's personal circumstances from that imaginary conversation. I thought of these things as I left the parking lot, turning my walk into a dissimulated jog.

"Morning, Max" the pissed voice calls me from behind, as it walks closer. "Oh! Morning, boss" the ill-prepared voice I happen to have replies as in a trance. "You heard all that?" the pissed voice that fakes a chuckle first, then forces itself to not appear too pissed, asks. "Uh? What do you mean?" the screaming-for-help voice I wished I never had, stammers while auditioning for a role in a mini-series nobody in the history of ever will see. Evangeline lets out a real chuckle this time.

"Nothing." A sigh or a deep breath or a physiological showcase of resignation. "How was your weekend?" my boss wonders, adjusting her pace to that of short-legged, tiny little me. "Nothing crazy. My frat had a party on Saturday, and I had a date on Sunday." That should leave room for justifying all the heavy-lifting I do for Sigma Phi Epsilon every time a party comes about, and for getting intel from an older woman on what works during a Sunday coffee double-date where the girl is more interested on Sam and Katie than on Max (me) and the idea of dating Max (also me). But Evangeline just whispers "Oh" and keeps walking, always adjusting her pace.

"Some people from Northeastern pulled up, it was great!" A nod and a second "Oh", ever so shyer. "They are always so unserious I swear." The 'um' that doesn't require you parting lips. "Even the girls jumped onto the pool from the roof!" A turn of the head towards me, while uttering "ow" and frowning. "But super helpful with the after, you know. Most stayed over the night and helped me clean up." The look of someone listening to someone somewhere making sounds with their mouth, none of them concatenating. "Otherwise, I'd been late to the date. Well, it was a double date. Sam and Katie wanted to see the coffee shop too." A subtle smile at either the word 'coffee', the mention of Sam and Katie or the concept of me going on a coffee date. "Absolute fiasco, boss. You'd never believe it." I exaggerated a head shake.

"You need to tell me more during the next tennis tournament" Evangeline worded it as an order desperately trying to be a silly invitation. I laughed with the eyes closed. "Yeah, sure. So today's the day... I get to meet all the staff." From the library entrance, I can already hear more noise than 'normal'; as I like to call my first six days on the job. "You'll see they are pretty friendly. I'm sure you'll get along. Oh, look! Rocio's on the front desk."
